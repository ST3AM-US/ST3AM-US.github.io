How it works

Three layers of verification, each catching a different class of denial.

Deterministic rules

~70% of denials. Eligibility (270/271), CDT code validity, frequency limits, attachment requirements, filing windows, tooth/surface validation, provider credentialing.

Language understanding

~20% of denials. Narrative quality scoring, narrative-to-code consistency, payer medical-necessity matching, COB sequencing.

Image verification (coming soon)

~10% of denials. Partnership-based vision AI on radiographs to confirm clinical findings match procedures billed.
